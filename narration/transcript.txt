MOVEMENT 06 / 12 · A first circle, not a hierarchy · 0:48
The First Circle proposal puts equal dignity before mechanism. One consenting, privately verified human contributor has one voice: not one wallet, one token balance, or one donation size. Music and code are equal contributions, and so are art, development, translation, sound and stewardship. Invitations and attendance do not create membership. IN, LATER, and NO are all valid answers, and silence is not consent. When the community asks for funding, the proposal is to ask for the whole circle and share equally by default, unless the circle openly decides otherwise. A restricted grant still follows its own stated terms; equal sharing does not rewrite them. There is no governance-token sale, no rank to purchase, no payment required to enter, and no promise of a grant, award, or income.
