    Glossary for newcomers

    DAO (proposal)
    A decentralized autonomous organization — people coordinating through shared, open rules. The Open Source Orchestra DAO is a proposal being talked through in these circles; nothing is on-chain, adopted or funded.
    Consent-first recording
    Consent is asked out loud on the recording itself; nothing goes public until everyone has said yes and the transcript has been checked. Takedown first, questions second.
    Open Source Orchestra
    An open circle of musicians, builders and listeners: no ticket, no conductor, every voice counts. It has played on the floor of Ethereum gatherings since Devcon 6 in Bogotá (2022).
    First names only
    Public editions use first names or chosen display names; surnames, contacts and private circumstances are removed.
