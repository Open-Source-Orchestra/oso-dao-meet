    How the index is made

    scripts/build_watch.py reads every published transcript (meetings/N/transcript/transcript.json, data/transcripts/*.json, and checked whisper drafts) and writes data/search-index.json. Gated or pending circles are never indexed. Takedown first: opensourceorchestra.org/takedown/.
