ACTUAL · EXPLORATORY OPENING

A first proposal circle, not a founding decision

Shaka opened this recorded segment as a first DAO-proposal and post-Devcon 8 retrospective gathering, with introductions and an invitation to explore ideas. The conversation was exploratory rather than a formal governance proceeding.

PRINCIPLE + VISION

Music as an open door and sanctuary

Participants described a welcoming, permissionless musical space grounded in care, safe hospitality, shared stewardship and respect for the people who make each moment possible. This is a cultural intention, not a newly adopted legal or governance rule.

PROPOSED RECOGNITION MODEL

Many kinds of contribution matter

Musical performance, code, production, audiovisual work, organizing, hosting and supportive presence were all discussed as meaningful. Equal recognition was explored; no membership list, score, allocation or formal entitlement was approved.

PROPOSAL ONLY

A humble DAO experiment

The host explicitly left open whether any token or particular voting mechanism would be appropriate. Consensus, decentralization, harmony and open participation were emphasized. No architecture, voting system, token launch or on-chain deployment was selected.

PROPOSAL ONLY

Sustainable care for contributors

Participants discussed ways to reduce practical barriers: advance resources, accommodation, travel, basic costs and a feasible core-plus-volunteer approach for events. No person was approved for payment or reimbursement.

POSSIBLE FUTURE PROPOSALS

Retroactive support after a future threshold

One idea was to consider Devcon 8-era contributions and costs after a future funding threshold, then move toward support for the road to Devcon 9. No threshold, eligibility rule, amount, recipient, treasury or distribution was finalized.

PROPOSALS + EXPRESSED INTEREST

Lisbon, shared equipment and hybrid continuity

Earlier Ethereum gatherings were discussed as possible steps toward Lisbon. A community-stewarded backline, local participation, remote/virtual contribution and recurring weekly conversations were explored. Event invitations, equipment commitments and future call times are not confirmed.

RECAP + FUTURE IDEAS

Open-source artist infrastructure

The host and Utkarsh recapped hackathon-era technical work involving ENS and agent-mediated tipping; participants imagined transparent artist-support tools and agent stewards. These notes do not independently verify performance, production readiness, security or claimed milestones. Future capabilities are aspirations, not deployed commitments.

Levi described an existing co-op approach to rights and transparent revenue sharing, then raised possible adaptations. Revenue-token and patron-style mechanisms were floated with uncertainty expressed. No rights assignment, split, token model, release, wallet request or Open Source Orchestra adoption is established; separate contributor consent and rights review would be necessary.
