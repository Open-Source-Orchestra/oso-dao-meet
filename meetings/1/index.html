What the circle is proposing

  • Every contribution is equal: music, code, art, development, translation, sound and stewardship.
  • One consenting, privately verified human contributor = one voice. No donation-weighted influence and no governance-token sale.
  • A public community ledger: proposals, answers, awards, splits, objections and execution receipts in the open.
  • Consent before exposure: names, faces, wallets, recordings, transcripts and attributed words each require their own appropriate yes.
  • Agents assist, never rule: AI may research, draft, check and mirror; no votes, keys, Shamans or autonomous execution.

What this is not

  • Not an on-chain DAO, deployed treasury, token launch or investment.
  • Not automatic membership from an invite, RSVP, photograph, artist card or meeting attendance.
  • Not a final charter, a completed governance vote or a treasury/asset migration.
  • Not a promise of award, payment, venue, Devcon endorsement or future Safe configuration.
  • Not a release of attendee lists, private Meet links, contact mappings or raw meeting media.

The path remains a proposal

  1. Listen: reconcile IN / LATER / NO equally; silence is never a yes.
  2. Practice: deliberate with a community Git ledger, privately verified humans and equal off-chain signalling.
