What the circle talked about

  • Stewarding an open sanctuary that feels like a living room, open to everybody and to all faiths, with people always free to leave. Shaka sees himself as a steward, not a conductor, and invited others to share that care. Ben said open circles still need structure, and that it is fine to ask someone gently not to play over you; Shaka offered a positive reframe, such as loving it when someone harmonizes with him.
  • Funding and transparency. Shaka said travel was reimbursed by the EF Road to Devcon while the music itself has been self-funded and underfunded. He wants receipts for any big spending, and no monetizing of the orchestra. Alaska raised Waterfall funding; Shaka said he will learn about it.
  • Tools and offers. Subcult came up as a place for streaming and building tools together. Alaska offered a plaza spot and connections in Barcelona’s music scene, and said the mixer is still in Barcelona. A studio was offered, with Consensus venues mentioned, FireEyes on board, and a guest room open to OSA guests.
  • Growing the DAO. Ben suggested quarterly milestones, three- and five-year plans, virtual rehearsals, announced events, Web3 in schools, and songs — including a rap with “L2, consensus, ZK proof words,” perhaps launching at Cannes on the way to Lisbon. Shaka said “Let's do it.” Possible stops mentioned include Cairns, New York and Lisbon. Shaka said he is confident about 2027, that his first single is coming, and that he plans a podcast with Griff as the first guest.
  • The join site and profiles. Shaka shared the public join page. Ben praised the site, asked about a duplicate profile, and was invited to claim an ENS name.

Shaka closed by singing “butterflies and love,” calling the space decentralized, permissionless and open source.

What was offered, not decided

Ideas voiced here — Waterfall funding, reputation or staking experiments, school visits, a Cannes-to-Lisbon song, venue pitches — remain proposals and hopes. The Open Source Orchestra DAO is still a proposal and public community ledger, not an adopted or on-chain DAO. No token sale, treasury move, or membership decision is claimed.
