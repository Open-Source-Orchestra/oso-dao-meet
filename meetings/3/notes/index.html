Stewarding an open sanctuary

Shaka wants the orchestra to feel like a living room open to everybody and to all faiths, a place for healing and radical self-expression, never policing. He sees himself as a steward, not a conductor, and invited everyone to share stewarding. People must always be free to leave. Ben said open circles still need structure, and that it is fine to tell someone gently not to sing or play over you. Shaka proposed a positive reframe, such as “I love it when you harmonize with me.” Both remembered Mumbai as deeply open, with kids joining on mic.

at 1:26:25 in the transcript

Funding and transparency

Shaka said travel was reimbursed by the EF Road to Devcon while the music itself has been self-funded, and that the work is underfunded. He wants transparent funding, with receipts for any big spending, and no monetizing of the orchestra. Alaska raised Waterfall funding, and Shaka said he will learn about it. A reputation-based system with early staking and a normal sign-up, possibly through sub-cults, was floated as an idea only.

at 0:40:58 in the transcript

Tools, venues and offers

Subcult came up as a dream platform for streaming and building tools together. Alaska offered a plaza spot and connections to Barcelona’s music scene, and said the mixer is still in Barcelona. A studio was offered, with Consensus venues mentioned. FireEyes was said to be on board, and OSA guests were welcomed to a guest room. Shaka mentioned storage, more instruments coming, and pitching venues with instruments brought along. He framed the orchestra as a front door for gigs, separate from solo DJing and side parties, and said anyone may represent it.

at 0:23:01 in the transcript

Growing the DAO, schools and songs

Ben suggested quarterly milestones, three- and five-year plans, virtual rehearsals, announced events and community suggestions. He wants Web3 in schools and spoke about a Web3 music platform and a rap with “L2, consensus, ZK proof words,” perhaps launching at Cannes on the way to Lisbon. Shaka said “Let's do it.” Shaka shared a kids’ hackathon where four kids built on Ethereum, and proposed school visits. Possible stops mentioned include Cairns, New York and Lisbon. Shaka said he is confident about 2027, that his first single is coming, and that he plans a podcast with Griff as the first guest.

at 0:30:32 in the transcript

The join site and profiles

Shaka shared the public join page and a public artist profile. Ben praised the site, wants a better photo, noticed a duplicate profile, and asked whether he needs to reapply or join the DAO. Shaka suggested claiming an ENS name.

at 1:10:00 in the transcript

Shaka closed by singing “butterflies and love,” calling the space decentralized, permissionless and open source, and thanking Googly Eye for transcribing.
