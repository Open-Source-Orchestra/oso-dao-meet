OSODAO.ORG: No throne. Just a circle.

● ● ●osodao.org / concept studynot a live service

Open Source Orchestra · a public circle

No throne.
Just a circle.

An orchestra score meets a round table. Every contribution has a place. Every consenting human has one voice. The center is not a ruler—it is the work we share.

Read the ground rules ↘Why no wallet? ↓

01 / VOICE

One consenting human.
One voice.

Participation is chosen, not presumed. Code and care, music and translation: each contribution matters.

02 / RECORD

A community ledger.
Currently not on-chain.

Public notes can be accountable without pretending that a blockchain or token has already been chosen.

03 / COMMONS

Shared equally
by default.

Funding asks are for the whole circle. There is no token weight, rank, or special seat at the table.

04 / CONSENT

No wallet ask.
No hidden enrollment.

The first invitation is to read and listen. Any future step needs clear consent and a human explanation.

osodao.com → osodao.org

One front door. The .com is an alias, never a separate brand.

Why the score?

A score holds difference without flattening it. A circle makes the same promise spatially: every seat can see every other seat, and the center belongs to the shared work.

A separate room stays separate.

The existing osodaomeet hall remains its own immersive room. This public-circle concept does not overwrite it, absorb it, or turn it into a membership funnel.

Abstract contours · not a cultural motif

Begin with an intention.

Four gentle prompts. No feed, no status performance.

Make something

Bring a half-formed idea. Find a table, a tool, or another curious pair of hands.

Read together

Leave a margin note. Share a book. Let a good question travel further than a hot take.

Share a meal

Offer a hand

Small help is a kind of infrastructure. Start with what you can genuinely offer.

Suggested build order · slow is smooth

Draw the circle
before the table.

The smallest trustworthy sequence starts with meaning, not machinery. Each stage can stop without leaving a hollow product behind.

  1. Bless the boundaries.

    Confirm the circle’s principles, the alias-only role of osodao.com, and the separate identity of the existing meet hall.

  2. Make the public circle legible.

    Start with osodao.org: plain-language principles, accessible notes, consent, and truthful status. No membership theater.

  3. Add the alias; prototype the table later.

    Point osodao.com to the canonical home only after it exists. Test osodao.app as a consent-first design prototype before any real decisions.

  4. Localize the porch with local stewards.

    Shape zuchiangmai.app with the people and tools already present. Link out more than it rebuilds; keep the live agenda and PIT work separate.

Non-negotiables / before any build

A door should not become a trap.

  • Consent before participation.
  • No invented activity or members.
  • No token, rank, or wallet gate.
  • Keyboard, mobile, reduced-motion, and no-JS access.
  • Honest labels for every prototype.
  • A visible, humane takedown path.

Keep the immersive room immersive. osodaomeet remains a distinct hall with its own artistic language. The public circle may eventually link to it, but must not replace it.
